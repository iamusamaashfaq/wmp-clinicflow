CARE WITHOUT THE WAIT

Clear, calm and connected.

ClinicFlow helps patients and reception teams coordinate a visit with less uncertainty. Our identity combines clinical clarity with a welcoming, human tone.

The care loop

A white care cross sits inside two directional arcs. The cross represents care; the loop represents patient-to-reception coordination and recovered appointment slots. The rounded silhouette makes it legible in small digital spaces.

Space and scale

  • Leave clear space of at least one cross-arm width around the mark.
  • Recommended icon size: 32 px or larger; minimum 24 px.
  • Use the horizontal logo at 180 px or wider.
  • Use the icon alone for favicons and compact navigation.

Keep the mark consistent

  • Keep original proportions and rounded corners.
  • Use the reversed version on dark surfaces.
  • Use teal rather than red for the care symbol.
  • Do not stretch, rotate, add effects or place on busy photographs.

Keep most of the interface white or mist. Use teal for the primary action and mint for quiet reassurance. Use dark text on pale surfaces; do not rely on colour alone to communicate status.

HEADINGS · POPPINS
A calmer clinic visit.

Poppins SemiBold 600 for page headings, section titles and buttons. Use sentence case, generous spacing and short, direct labels.

Page heading 30–48 px · Section heading 20–24 px · Button 12–14 px
BODY · OPEN SANS

Your original appointment stays safe until you accept an earlier visit.

Open Sans Regular 400 for body text and instructions; 600 for field labels and emphasis. Both families are self-hosted and shipped with their SIL Open Font License files.

Body 14–16 px · Supporting text 12–13 px · Line height 1.6–1.75

Sound helpful. Stay precise.

Say this

“An earlier appointment is available.”
“Your original booking stays safe.”
“Estimated wait: 20–35 minutes.”

Product principles

Show confirmation after every booking action. Explain what happens next. Describe estimates as estimates. Keep medical decisions with clinical staff.
